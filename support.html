Support

Practical answers, with room for the details.

How do I scan across dates?

Open Scan Options at the top right of Similar Photos. Choose the last 30 days, all dates, or a custom date range, then scan that range to compare photos across dates. Analysed features are cached on the device; repeat scans reuse unchanged photos and analyse new or edited content. Use the full rescan option to ignore the cache when needed. The network-enabled scan can download unavailable iCloud content. Scanning never deletes photos automatically.

How do I browse the private vault?

Unlock the vault and open an item, then swipe horizontally through photos, Live Photos, and videos. The top shows your position and total count. Export, save, and delete icons stay fixed on the media page; use the information button for details.

How do I change photo access?

iOS asks for photo permission when needed. You can select only some photos. Adjust the selection or permission in iOS Settings for this app. Website access requires no photo permission.

Can I undo a deletion?

Review marked photos before submitting. Actual deletion requires system confirmation. Similar photos are submitted one group at a time; cancellation or failure keeps the current choices. After successful deletion, check Photos → Recently Deleted. Apple normally keeps deleted items for 30 days. iCloud Photos deletion may sync to other devices. Back up first.

What can the private vault import?

The vault supports photos, Live Photos, and videos, preserving Live Photo motion and video sound. Export original resources or save them to Photos. Import does not remove system originals. Keep originals and verify exported or restored content. Complete round trips for RAW, edit history, or every associated resource are not promised.

How do private backups work?

Unlock the vault and export an encrypted backup. Store the backup and its randomly generated recovery code separately and securely. Operations require enough free device storage. If storage or resource-reading errors occur, follow the message and retry. Restore with the matching code and verify the result. Lost codes cannot be recovered. Recovery from every system or device failure cannot be guaranteed.

Are location details removed from exports?

Exports may retain original metadata, including location. There is currently no remove-location sharing option. Check the file and destination privacy settings before sharing.

My purchase has not appeared. What should I do?

Use the Apple account that made the purchase and select Restore Purchases in the app. Missing products or network problems do not produce invented prices or success states. If needed, provide the app version, iOS version, and error description; never send full payment details.

How do I change language or ad choices?

The app follows the system language and supports iOS per-app language settings. If ads are enabled in the released version, use the in-app privacy choices entry to manage applicable consent. Pro shows no ads.
