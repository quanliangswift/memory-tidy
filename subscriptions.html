Subscriptions

Understand the options before you decide.

Monthly Pro

If offered in the app, the monthly option is an auto-renewable subscription. Unless cancelled, it renews at the period and local price shown by Apple. Amount, currency, taxes, availability, and offer eligibility depend on live StoreKit information and Apple’s confirmation screen. This website lists no demo prices.

One-time Pro

If offered in the app, the one-time option purchases the Pro entitlements listed in the app without monthly auto-renewal. It does not mean perpetual cloud services, unlimited storage, or all future features. Check the app’s entitlements at purchase.

Entitlements and ads

Pro shows no ads. The app lists other current entitlements and free limits. Viewing, exporting original files, and restoring existing vault content are not paywalled when Pro expires; new-item limits follow the current version. Purchases may be unavailable while products are unconfigured or cannot load.

Payment, renewal, and cancellation

Apple handles payment and renewal as shown in its confirmation flow. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling renewal generally leaves access through the paid period. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel ahead of the deadline Apple provides to avoid another renewal.

Restore and refunds

Use the same Apple account and Restore Purchases in the app to recheck entitlements. Restoring purchases does not restore unbacked-up private photos. Apple handles refunds under its current policy; the developer cannot guarantee a refund.
