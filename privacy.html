Photos and on-device processing

iOS controls photo access. Choose limited or full access and change it in Settings. Photo review, similarity analysis, and review progress are processed and stored on your device; we do not collect or upload photos through our own servers. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple synchronisation depends on your system settings and Apple terms.

Private vault and backups

Static photo copies and the vault index are encrypted locally with AES-GCM. Keychain and device authentication protect the key; the app does not receive your Face ID biometric data. Private content is covered and locked in the background, but screenshots or external photography cannot be prevented. Import currently leaves originals in Photos. Files you choose to export or share may retain metadata such as location; recipients and destination services apply their own rules.

Retention, deletion, and control

Review progress and vault files are stored on the device. Use the app’s available controls to manage content. Uninstalling may lose local data and does not automatically delete exported backups or system photos. Export and verify backups first. An external encrypted backup requires its matching recovery code; a lost code cannot be recovered. Contact the configured email below for privacy assistance. Never send photos or recovery codes.

Purchases and third parties

Apple StoreKit handles purchases and restoration. The app reads verified transaction and entitlement status to provide Pro. We do not receive full payment card details. Apple handles transactions under its own privacy policy.

App advertising (conditional)

App ads are disabled in this configuration. This status must match the shipped app.

The app can conditionally enable Google AdMob. The package includes the Google advertising SDK, which may run bootstrap validation even while ads are disabled; disabling ads does not remove the SDK. When ads are requested, Google may process IP addresses, device information, diagnostics and performance data, ad interactions, and device or advertising identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Details depend on the SDK, configuration, region, and consent choices. Required regional consent is requested first; use the app’s privacy choices to review it. Cross-app tracking also requires iOS permission where applicable. Pro shows no ads. Ads do not access photos or vault content, and photos are not used for ad targeting.

Adults and family use

This product is for adults, including parents, and is not offered as a Kids Category app. It does not create child identity profiles or ask for a child’s name, birth date, or identity information. Do not send child identity details in support emails.

This website and updates

This website embeds no ads, analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, forms, or third-party fonts and sets no analytics cookies. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process IP addresses and access logs to serve it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Policy changes will update the effective date.
